Common Questions

Is a license required to repair irrigation systems in Texas?

Yes. Texas requires irrigation work to be performed or directly supervised by a TCEQ Licensed Irrigator. Journey One Landscape Team holds Texas Licensed Irrigator license LI0027872, issued by the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality.

How much does irrigation repair cost in San Antonio?

Most repairs range from $75 to $300 depending on the issue. Head replacements are at the lower end of that range; valve repairs, line breaks and zone rebuilds are priced by scope. Estimates are free and provided before any work begins.

How quickly can you schedule service?

We offer same-day response Monday through Saturday, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Call or text (210) 307-8226, or request an appointment online.

Do you repair backflow preventers?

Yes. As a TCEQ Licensed Irrigator, we repair and replace backflow preventers in compliance with SAWS requirements.

What areas do you serve?

We serve San Antonio, Helotes, Leon Valley, Converse, Schertz, Universal City, Live Oak and Windcrest. Please contact us to confirm service at your address.
